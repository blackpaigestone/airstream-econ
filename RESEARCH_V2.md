# The Economics of Optionality — research extension

## Central question
Has the cost of maintaining economic freedom increased faster than the purchasing power of income?

## Three complementary questions
1. Does an equal real income gain create equal progress toward independence across generations? **Status:** illustrative calculator only; age/household-specific historical data not yet assembled.
2. Has the cost of preserving a reference set of options increased relative to earnings? **Status:** transparent reference basket; no historical flexibility index estimated yet.
3. When does asset ownership outweigh the opportunity costs of geographic commitment? **Status:** five-year and transition-cost scenarios, not observed causal estimates.

## Five-year objective
Maximize future opportunities while preserving freedom to choose: homeownership, international relocation, continued travel, or business building. Model remote field employment as primary net income and artist-representation business income separately (default $0). Business projections are not verified.

## Methodological boundaries
- CPI is a price-level index; inflation is its growth rate.
- MEHOINUSA672N is already a **real** income series; never deflate again.
- CPI and real median income are independently indexed to 1990=100. This is a descriptive comparison, not a real wage or affordability ratio.
- The vehicle five-year calculation is nominal, assumes 100% financing, fixed 8% APR/15 years, no investment return, 5% resale transaction costs, and excludes acquisition taxes, insurance detail, personal taxes, medical costs, currency conversion, and business startup capital unless included in user-selected expenses.
- Negative liquid balance means a financing shortfall, not viable wealth.
- Remote work from Canada is subject to employer, immigration, tax and insurance restrictions.
- Historical research still requires BLS Consumer Expenditure Survey, Census age-specific income and housing, SCF liquidity, regional wage/price data, and independently observed vehicle comps.
